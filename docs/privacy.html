Privacy Policy

Application: Olympus View · Last updated: 2026-05-31

Olympus View is a free, open-source application that lets you view, download, and delete photos on Olympus and OM System cameras over WiFi. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OM Digital Solutions, Olympus Corporation, or OM System. "Olympus" and "OM System" are trademarks of their respective owners.

Summary

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • All communication happens directly between your device and your camera over your local WiFi.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs inside the app.

Data the app handles on your device

  • Photos and videos downloaded from your camera are saved to your device's gallery (e.g. DCIM/OlympusView on Android) or to a folder you choose. They never leave your device through this app.
  • Connection history (camera SSID and password) is stored locally using Android SharedPreferences so you can reconnect quickly. It never leaves your device.
  • Thumbnail cache is stored in the app's private cache directory and can be cleared from the system settings at any time.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera — to scan the QR code shown on your Olympus / OM System camera screen and parse the WiFi credentials. The camera stream is processed on-device and not stored or transmitted.
  • Location (Fine / Coarse) — Android requires this permission for apps that programmatically connect to or scan WiFi networks. We do not read, store, or transmit your geographic location.
  • WiFi state and change — to connect your device to the camera's WiFi access point.
  • Internet / Network state — to talk HTTP to the camera at 192.168.0.10 over the local WiFi. No other network destinations are contacted by the app.
  • Storage (legacy, Android 9 and older) — to write downloaded photos to the shared gallery on older Android versions.

Network access

The app makes plain HTTP requests only to the fixed local address 192.168.0.10 (the camera's WiFi access point), enforced by an Android Network Security Configuration. No remote servers are contacted.

Third-party services

The application does not embed analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly process any personal data of children.

Open source

Source code is publicly available, so anyone can verify the claims above: github.com/dpolarov/olympus-view-and-delete.
